Vibration effects
Choose which force feedback effects trigger phone vibration:
NORMAL: plain steering, the wheel springs back to centre when you let go. FFB: the game's force feedback turns the wheel (no auto-centring, as on a real wheel) and shakes the phone. Tip: add &ffb=1 to the bookmark to start in FFB mode.
Forza Mode
FORZA HORIZON
Enables Forza Horizon compatibility: inverts pedal axes (0=full press, max=released) to match the GameInput RacingWheel descriptor (vjoy.reg), maps handbrake to a wheel button, adds top-bar Map, Rewind, View, and Pause controls, and configures Forza wheel buttons (Cancel, Accept, Gear Up/Down). Tip: add &forza=1 to the bookmark to start in Forza mode.
